Play the original SimAnt with a friend. Share a colony, control your own ant, and explore with separate views.

Start with Quick Game for tested co-op. Full Game founding uses the original controls. Co-op combat and long campaigns are still experimental.

The host runs the original game. Player two joins beside them in the current patch.

Connection help

Keep the host’s game open and visible. On Android, allow Nearby devices / Local network in Settings → Apps → your browser → Permissions. Also allow any local-network permission requested by the browser.

Some networks need a TURN relay. You can supply one below; credentials stay in this tab.
